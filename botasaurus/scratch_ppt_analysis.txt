SLIDE 9

Limitations, Challenges & Risks
Data Quality Limitations
• Email addresses: 30-35% annual decay (self-reported in registries)
• Catch-all domains: 40% of mid-market domains accept all email (requires Bayesian scoring)
• Executive data: "Lawyer as Executive" problem (need NLP filtering for legal reps)
• Address standardization: 80+ state aliases need normalization
Technical Challenges
• Government servers: Frequent downtime, high latency, unannounced maintenance
• Legacy systems: SIGER 2.0 uses JSF architecture (requires stateful crawling)
• WAF/Bot detection: Cloudflare & DataDome on commercial sites (proxy costs)
• Scale: SMTP validation at 5.5M+ records requires IP rotation to avoid blacklisting
Operational Risks
• API changes: Unannounced schema/layout changes break parsers (need monitoring)
• Regulatory shifts: INAI potential restructuring; LFPDPPP modernization possible
• Compliance: PROFECO/REPEP telemarketing restrictions (B2B exempt but requires verification)
• Cost scaling: Proxy bandwidth & cloud infrastructure costs at 5.5M+ records

Validation Methodology: 4-Stage Email Verification
1
Syntax & RFC 5322
Regex validation, discard role accounts (abuse@, postmaster@)
2
DNS MX Lookup
Async DNS check for active Mail Exchange records
3
SMTP Handshake
Port 25 socket: HELO → MAIL FROM → RCPT TO (connection terminated immediately)
4
Catch-All Detection
Probe with randomized string; Bayesian score for confidence
SMTP Response Interpretation:
250 OK
Mailbox confirmed active ✓
550 User Unknown
Email does not exist ✗
421/450 Greylisting
Retry with exponential backoff ↻
